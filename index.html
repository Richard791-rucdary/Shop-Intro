What is Jotter?

Jotter is a website created by Rucdary basically for the purpose of effectively storing debtors' data with ease.

Why would i prefer Jotter to my handwritten credit book?

Jotter provides a variety of advantages compared to handwritten credit books such as:

  • Portability: Portable mobile devices like phones, can be carried anywhere. no need to carry heavy books anymore.


  • Massive Storage: Hundreds to Thousands of data can be stored in one account. way more than any book can hold.


  • Flexible editing: Records can be cleanly recorded, edited and deleted. No messy scripts.


  • Faster data lookup: Data lookup is faster in hundreds of records. Saves time + effort


  • Data Independency: Data is independently stored in our Databases.. Therefore it can't be lost if you loose your device.

    Manual credit book lost = data gone.

    Phone lost = just log back into your account and you get your data back.
